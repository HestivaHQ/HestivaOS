# Customer financial disclosure policy

- Approved: 2026-08-13

Material financial terms must be communicated explicitly during quotation, booking, confirmation, and applicable account correspondence.

When actual rand amounts are known, communication must show those amounts rather than percentages alone. Example: a R1,460 service should be capable of being shown as R730 pay now and R730 balance after service.

For recurring customers, communication must clearly explain:

- normal visit price;
- amount already held in advance;
- balance attributable to the completed visit;
- advance allocated to the next visit;
- amount payable now;
- next scheduled visit; and
- cancellation/rescheduling consequences.

For approved month-end customers, communication must additionally show:

- selected billing date;
- completed services in the active billing cycle;
- running amount;
- expected amount due;
- standing security/advance; and
- next payment date.

Recurring service remains month-to-month with no fixed-term lock-in and requires at least 14 days' notice to end or pause. Individual visit cancellation/rescheduling remains under the approved 24-hour policy. Deposits/advances and cancellation charges are separate concepts.

Standard recurring customers maintain a standing advance equal to 50% of one normal visit. After each completed standard recurring visit, payment settles the outstanding portion and replenishes the next-visit advance. An unpaid previous applicable balance prevents another clean from proceeding.
